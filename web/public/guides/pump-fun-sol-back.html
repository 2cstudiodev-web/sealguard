Trading on pump.fun leaves SOL locked in two places. Neither is lost: you can close both and the SOL comes back to your wallet.

1. The volume-account deposit

On your first trade, pump.fun opens a volume account for your wallet, and PumpSwap opens another. Each holds about 0.0018 SOL, plus any SOL cashback you haven't claimed.

  • Claim first. If the account still holds pump.fun rewards, or cashback paid in a token such as USDC, claim those on pump.fun first. SealGuard won't offer to close it until you have.
  • It comes back. Your next trade on pump.fun opens a new one and takes the deposit again, so close it only if you've stopped trading there.

2. Empty token accounts

Every memecoin you bought has its own token account holding rent. Accounts opened before September 2026 hold about 0.002 SOL each; newer ones about 0.0015 SOL, after Solana lowered rent. Selling the coin empties the account but doesn't close it. Fifty sold memecoins come to about 0.075–0.1 SOL.

How SealGuard closes them

Connect your wallet, check the list (tokens you'll likely receive again and accounts used in the last 30 days are left unticked, with the reason), and press Fix selected. Your wallet shows each transaction before you sign.

Fee: 5% of the deposit or rent returned, paid in the same transaction; not on your cashback. Wallets with the guard (US$49 a year) pay no fee.

Questions

Does closing touch my coins?

No. Only accounts with a zero balance can be closed.

Can I do it for free?

Yes, with the Solana command line (spl-token close for token accounts). See closing empty token accounts.
