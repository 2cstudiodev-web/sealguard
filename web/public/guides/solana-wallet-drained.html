Solana wallet drained? What to do in the first hour

If tokens or SOL left your wallet without you sending them, act in this order. The goal is to stop further losses first, then find out how they got in.

Check my wallet for what they can still do

Free and read-only. It shows approvals, authorities and account changes an attacker may have left behind.

1. Stop signing anything

Don't approve another transaction or message from the site that started this, and close it. If a wallet pop-up appears that you didn't expect, reject it.

2. Work out how they got in

  • They have your seed phrase or private key (you typed it into a site, a "support" chat, or a file on an infected computer): assume they can take everything at any time. Go to step 3 now.
  • You signed something on a website: they may only have the permissions that transaction gave them. Step 4 shows what those are.

If you can't tell, treat it as the first case.

3. If the seed phrase is exposed: move what's left

Create a brand-new wallet with a new seed phrase (not a new account under the same phrase), and send what's left to it. Start with what's worth most. Don't import the old phrase anywhere again.

4. Check what they can still do

Scan the wallet. SealGuard lists:

  • Token approvals: an address that can still move a token without asking you. Revoke them; it's free.
  • Stake and nonce accounts someone else controls: an attacker who changed the authority can withdraw them later.
  • A taken-over wallet account: if the wallet account itself was reassigned to a program, it's lost; move everything else at once.
  • Phishing tokens that led you to the site: don't open their links; burn or ignore them.

5. Ignore "recovery" offers

People who reply to your post offering to get the funds back, and "recovery" sites that ask you to connect or sign, are a second scam. Transactions on Solana can't be reversed by anyone.

Questions

Can SealGuard get my tokens back?

No. Nobody can reverse a Solana transaction. SealGuard shows what an attacker can still do so you can close it off, and moves nothing without your signature.

Is it safe to scan a drained wallet?

Yes. Pasting the address only reads public chain data; you don't need to connect.
