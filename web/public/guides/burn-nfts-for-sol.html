Burn NFTs to get SOL back on Solana

A regular Solana NFT is three accounts: the token account, the metadata and the edition. All three hold rent. Burning the NFT closes them and the rent comes back to your wallet.

Check my wallet for NFTs

Free scan. Nothing is burnt until you tick it and approve it in your wallet.

How much comes back

About 0.006 SOL per NFT in our tests (18 NFTs in one wallet, about 0.006 SOL each). Metaplex keeps a small protocol fee inside the metadata account, so a little less than the full rent comes back.

Which NFTs qualify

  • Yes: standard Metaplex NFTs and programmable NFTs (pNFTs) that you hold and that aren't frozen.
  • No: compressed NFTs (they hold no rent, so burning returns nothing), Metaplex Core NFTs (not supported yet), print editions, and NFTs that are frozen, for example while staked.

Before you burn

Burning can't be undone. Check the NFT's floor price on a marketplace first: if anyone would pay more than about 0.006 SOL for it, selling is better than burning. SealGuard never ticks an NFT for you, and asks you to confirm that you understand burning destroys it before the fix button works.

Phishing NFTs (named after a website or a "claim" message) are listed too, still unticked. Don't open their links; burning them removes them and returns the rent.

Fee: 5% of the SOL that comes back, paid in the same transaction. Wallets with the guard pay no fee.

Questions

Does burning affect anyone else?

No. Only the NFT you burn is destroyed; other holders' NFTs aren't touched.

Is it safe?

SealGuard only reads public data until you sign. Each burn is a transaction your wallet shows you first, and the code is public.
