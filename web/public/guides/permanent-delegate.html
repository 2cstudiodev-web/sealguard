Token-2022 permanent delegate: who can move your tokens without asking

Token-2022, Solana's newer token program, lets a token's creator name a permanent delegate: an address that can transfer or burn that token from any holder's wallet, without the holder's signature. Unlike an ordinary approval, it can't be revoked.

Check my wallet for permanent delegates

Free and read-only.

When it's normal

Regulated tokens use it on purpose: stablecoins such as PayPal USD, and tokenised stocks, so the issuer can comply with legal orders. A token like that has a market price and a known issuer. SealGuard reports it as a low-severity note, nothing to do unless you don't trust the issuer.

When it's a red flag

An unknown token with no market price and a permanent delegate means its creator can take it back or burn it at any time. If you paid for it, its value depends entirely on that creator. SealGuard reports it as medium severity, and can burn it from your wallet if you'd rather not hold it.

How to check

Scan your wallet. Each token with a permanent delegate shows the delegate's address, whether the token has a market price, and what you can do.

Questions

Can I remove a permanent delegate?

No. Only the token's design sets it. You can sell, swap or burn the token.

Does it affect my other tokens?

No. It applies only to that one token.
