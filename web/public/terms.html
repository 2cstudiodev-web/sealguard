What SealGuard is

SealGuard reads public Solana data about a wallet and points out approvals, token restrictions, phishing airdrops, stake and nonce authorities and empty accounts. It is general information, not financial, legal or security advice, and it can be wrong or incomplete: no scan finds everything.

You stay in control

SealGuard never asks for a seed phrase or private key and never holds one. It cannot send a transaction from your wallet. Every fix is a transaction your wallet shows you and you sign. You decide what to tick and what to sign, and you are responsible for those choices. Read each transaction before you sign: revoking, burning, selling and closing accounts can't be undone, and burnt tokens and NFTs are gone for good. SealGuard isn't responsible for losses from transactions you sign, including tokens or NFTs you choose to burn or sell, or from what other sites, apps or tokens do.

Selling small balances

Sales go through Jupiter, a swap service run by a third party; SealGuard doesn't set the price and doesn't control Jupiter. Amounts shown before you sign are estimates. The price can change between the quote and your signature, and a sale can return less than estimated, within the limit shown in your wallet, or fail. A token you sell may later be worth more. Any tax on a sale is your responsibility.

Fees

Scanning, revoking approvals, burning phishing tokens and withdrawing nonce accounts are free. Closing empty token accounts, burning NFTs and selling small balances carry a 5% fee on the SOL they return to you (for a sale, the least SOL the swap may return plus the account's rent), paid in the same transaction and shown in your wallet before you sign; wallets with an active guard pay no fee. Network fees are paid to Solana, not to us.

Transactions on Solana can't be reversed. Once a transaction you made through SealGuard is confirmed, including closing accounts, reclaiming rent, burning or any other fix, it is final, and any fee taken in it can't be refunded.

The guard

The guard costs US$49 a year for one wallet, paid in USDC, SOL or SKR to 2C Studio's wallet. It doesn't renew by itself: we message your linked Telegram a week before it ends, and you can extend it from the same wallet. Telegram is linked after you pay. The guard re-checks the wallet about once an hour and messages your linked Telegram when something changes for the worse, with a weekly all-clear and a monthly reminder when there is rent to claim back. It's a best-effort alert, not protection: it can't stop a transaction, alerts can be late or missed (for example if Telegram or our data provider is down), and it can't see thefts that are a single signed transfer. If the guard can't run for your wallet because of a fault on our side, write to us and we'll refund the unused part.
